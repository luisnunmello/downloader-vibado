Server settings

The largest file anyone can upload, the master included. Guests with a lower limit keep it. Files are streamed in chunks, so size is limited by free disk space, not memory. Behind a reverse proxy, raise the proxy's own upload limit too.

Guest uploaders

Guests sign in with their name and password, can upload, and can see and manage their own files only. Leave a limit empty for the default (size) or no limit (expiry, total storage). When a guest has a max expiry, their files cannot be permanent.
